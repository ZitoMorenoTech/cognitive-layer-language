# Principles & Intended Use

*A statement of intent from the author of CLL (Cognitive Layer Language).*

This document has **no legal force**. The license (Apache 2.0) is what governs your legal rights to use, modify, and distribute CLL. This is something different: a plain statement of why I built CLL, what I hope it is used for, and where I stand on uses I did not intend. I write it because the license controls the terms of use — it does not control the use itself — and I would rather state my intention openly than pretend a clause could enforce it.

---

## What CLL is

CLL is a system for making the **structure** of a language-model's output predictable and auditable. It organizes *form* — the shape, fields, order, and constraints of a response. It does not generate new capability, does not reason toward goals, and does not produce content that did not already exist in its input. It is a layer of structure, not a source of power.

This matters for everything below. A tool that organizes the form of a document is indifferent to the document's content. That indifference is CLL's strength as an engineering tool, and it is also the reason its responsible use depends on the person who wields it, not on the tool itself.

---

## Why I built it

I built CLL to make working with language models more reliable for ordinary, constructive work: structuring documents, extracting information into consistent fields, summarizing to a fixed schema, comparing options fairly, normalizing messy data. The kind of work that fills the day in research, education, healthcare administration, journalism, libraries, small businesses, and public services.

My hope is simple: that CLL is most useful in the peaceful, everyday work that returns value to the people who do it and to the society around them. That is the purpose it was designed for and the purpose I want it associated with.

---

## Uses I consider aligned with its purpose

CLL is domain-neutral by design. It helps wherever a task needs a precise output structure and the model would otherwise improvise inconsistently. Uses I consider fully aligned with its intent include, among many others:

- Structuring and extracting information from documents into consistent, auditable fields.
- Summarizing sources to a fixed schema without inventing or editorializing.
- Comparing options against identical criteria, fairly and reproducibly.
- Normalizing heterogeneous data into a canonical format.
- Producing consistent records, catalog entries, or reference cards.
- Verifying that a text meets a defined contract of form and content.

Across research, education, healthcare, finance, journalism, public administration, commerce, and knowledge management — the administrative and documentary work that any organization runs on.

---

## Where I stand on uses I did not intend

I want to be honest rather than reassuring.

Once CLL is published openly, I cannot control who uses it or for what. The Apache 2.0 license permits use by anyone, including commercial, governmental, and military actors, and I understand that a license clause would not stop anyone already willing to act outside the law. I chose openness deliberately, with that understanding.

So this section is not a prohibition — it cannot be one. It is a statement of values:

- CLL was **not** conceived as an instrument of harm, coercion, deception, surveillance for repression, or any use that damages people or their rights.
- I do **not** endorse such uses, and their possibility does not reflect the purpose for which CLL exists.
- Responsibility for how CLL is used rests with the person or organization that uses it. My responsibility is the decision to publish and the terms under which I do so — a decision I have made with awareness of the full range of possible uses, not only the ones I hope for.

I believe the value of an open, structural tool that helps constructive work returns more to society than the marginal misuse an open tool inevitably permits. That belief is the reason this project is open, and this document is where I record it.

---

## In short

CLL organizes form; it does not create capability. It is meant for the everyday, constructive work that returns value to the people who do it. I cannot control its use, and I do not claim to. What I can do is state plainly what I built it for — and this is that statement.
